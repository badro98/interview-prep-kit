# Stage 5 — Final Round · Values & Mutual Fit

> **Template doc** — customize for your final interviewer using `intel.md`.

## What they're assessing

- **Values and culture fit:** Will you thrive on this team?
- **Leadership readiness:** How you think about scope, mentorship, and impact
- **Mutual fit:** You're evaluating them too
- **Long-term motivation:** Why stay and grow here?

The technical bar is largely cleared — this round is about judgment, character, and alignment.

## Likely questions

- Why [COMPANY]? Why this role now?
- What are you looking for in a manager and team?
- Where do you see yourself in 2–3 years?
- What would you do in your first 90 days?
- What questions do you have for me?

## Talking points

- Lead with genuine enthusiasm tied to something specific (product, team, mission).
- Have a crisp first-90-days framework: learn → contribute → propose.
- Ask about team dynamics, success metrics, and what keeps them excited.

## Pitfalls to avoid

- Raising comp/equity unless they bring it up first.
- Sounding like you haven't researched the company or interviewer.
- No questions prepared — always have 3–5 ready.

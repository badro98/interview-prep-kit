## What they're assessing

- **Role fit:** Does your background match what they need?
- **Motivation:** Why this company and role — is it thoughtful or generic?
- **Communication:** Can you tell your story clearly in 2–3 minutes?
- **Logistics:** Timeline, comp expectations, work authorization.

## Likely questions

- Walk me through your background.
- Why are you interested in [COMPANY] / this role?
- What are you looking for in your next position?
- What's your timeline and availability?
- Salary expectations (may come later — know your range).

## Talking points

- Open with a tight 90-second arc: where you've been → what you're great at → why here.
- Name 1–2 things you know about the company that aren't on the homepage.
- Ask about the interview process and what success looks like in the first 90 days.

## Pitfalls to avoid

- Rambling past 3 minutes on "tell me about yourself."
- Badmouthing a previous employer.
- Being vague on why this role vs. any similar opening.
